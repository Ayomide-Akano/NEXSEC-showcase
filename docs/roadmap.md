# Roadmap

## Now

- Deepen conservative, evidence-based asset classification.
- Finish and live-test Continuous Watch controls: start, suspend, resume, stop, and status.
- Complete end-to-end verification of the Command Center inventory and detail flows.
- Continue testing firewall, segmentation, risk, incident, audit, and zero-trust capabilities.

## Next

- Linux/nftables gateway integration as the first full test of the chain: discover gateway → classify → authorize → provision access → validate capability → test a controlled action → record evidence.
- Baseline engine covering assets, services, relationships, and trust.
- Service intelligence covering exposure, authentication, versions, and weaknesses.
- Stronger lifecycle state management and recovery behavior.

## Later

- Anomaly detection against trusted baselines.
- Contextual risk scoring.
- Policy engine defining which actions require human approval and which may become autonomous.
- Role-aware reporting and security operations workflows.
- Hardening of NEXSEC itself.
- Distributed sensors and privacy-aware shared intelligence.
- AI-assisted analysis constrained by evidence, policy, authorization, and auditability.
- Intelligence Cloud and collective intelligence capabilities.
- Licensing and controlled enterprise deployment.

The roadmap deliberately prioritizes trustworthy automation over maximum automation.
